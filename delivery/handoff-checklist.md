# Client Delivery Handoff Checklist

Do not mark the setup delivered until every required item is checked.

## Configuration and compliance

- [ ] Confirm the client approved the business name, hours, daytime text, and after-hours text.
- [ ] Confirm the sender's A2P Brand and Campaign are approved and the Twilio number is attached to the correct Messaging Service/Campaign.
- [ ] Confirm STOP and HELP handling is active and the client knows what customer consent is being recorded.

## Live tests

- [ ] Test the text-back from **three different phones**.
- [ ] Verify an answered call does not trigger a missed-call text; test unanswered/busy and after-hours behavior.
- [ ] Confirm the **first message includes `Reply STOP to opt out`**.
- [ ] Reply STOP from a test phone and confirm it is opted out of further messages.
- [ ] Open the Google review link on a phone and confirm it reaches the correct business review page.
- [ ] Confirm the owner's mobile receives forwarded calls and can reply to customers.

## Client handoff

- [ ] Send the client the one-page [`How to turn it off`](how-to-turn-it-off.md) instruction sheet.
- [ ] Show the client how to contact the installer if they need to pause texting or change hours/messages.
- [ ] Get the client's sign-off that the live setup and customer-facing messages look right.
