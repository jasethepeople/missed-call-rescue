# Vertical Text-Back Scripts

All messages are under 160 characters and include the required vertical question plus `Reply STOP to opt out`.

## Plumbing

**Daytime — 78 characters**

> Sorry we missed you. Is this an emergency or scheduling? Reply STOP to opt out

**After-hours — 106 characters**

> We're closed. Is this an emergency or scheduling? We'll reply during business hours. Reply STOP to opt out

**Emergency / urgent — 85 characters**

> We prioritize urgent calls. Is this an emergency or scheduling? Reply STOP to opt out
